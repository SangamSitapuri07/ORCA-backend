Top 20 Old SIH Problem Statements That Were Never Really Finished — and How to Complete Them

SIH does not publish per-problem "solution quality" scores, so this list is built on hard signals mined from 4 years of problem-statement archives: (1) ministries re-submitting the same problem in multiple editions — the clearest sign no past solution was adopted, and (2) well-documented hackathon failure modes where every 36-hour prototype in that space fell short of a deployable solution.

2023: ~180 PS mined 2024: ~230 PS mined 2025: ~100 PS mined 2026 cross-check: 226 PS

How "incomplete / not up to mark" was judged

🔍 The two signals used (be skeptical of anything else — winner code is rarely public):

  • Signal 1 — Re-submission (strongest): If a ministry pays the cost of writing and mentoring the same problem again in 2024, 2025 or 2026, the previous winners' outputs were clearly not deployed/adopted. Several problems below have been asked 3 editions running.
  • Signal 2 — Structural incompleteness: Problems where the winning-typical approach is known to stop at a toy: CNN trained on 200 images, SOS button apps, slideshow "text-to-video", chatbots that hallucinate on legal text. The gap between demo and deployment is documented and closeable.
  • Bonus column: Where the unsolved 2023–25 problem has re-incarnated as an SIH 2026 PS, your completed work becomes directly competition-ready.

The 20 picks

Ordered by combination of hardness of the gap + impact if completed + evidence of repeated ask. Each card shows why past attempts fell short and the exact features that would make it "complete".

1
SIH 2023 · SIH1343 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2024 (×3 herbal PS)

Medicinal Plant / Raw-Drug Identification

Ministry of AYUSH

Why past attempts fell short

  • Everyone trained a leaf-CNN on 10–20 garden plants from phone photos
  • Real AYUSH supply chain needs raw drug ID: dried roots, bark, powders — nobody tackled it
  • No adulterant/substitute detection (the actual industry pain point)
  • No offline mode for field/forest collection

What "complete" looks like

  • Two-stage pipeline: fresh-plant vision model + raw-drug model with confidence + "unknown" rejection
  • Adulteration flag via texture/micro-feature comparison against reference samples
  • Offline-first Android app (TFLite) + Indic languages for collectors
  • Validated against a public herbarium/raw-drug atlas; publish accuracy per class
2026 echo: Ministry of Ayush is back with IP-SAKTI (SIH26045) & clinical-trial PS — domain trust is still being built; a drug-authentication tool slots right in.
2
SIH 2024 · SIH1555 / SIH1715 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2022, 2023, 2024

Indian Sign Language ⇄ Text/Speech Translation

Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment / AICTE

Why past attempts fell short

  • Nearly every team demoed static fingerspelling alphabets (ASL-style), not real ISL sentences
  • Zero handling of non-manual markers (facial grammar) — which the 2024 PS explicitly demanded
  • One-way only; no signed-video generation back from text

What "complete" looks like

  • Continuous sign recognition on ISL sentence datasets (pose-track + sequence models), not isolated gestures
  • Non-manual feature channel (eyebrows/mouthing) fused with hand features
  • Two-way: ISL→text and text→avatar/gloss output
  • Field-tested with a deaf association; word-error-rate reported honestly
Why now: Open pose-estimation + video models are finally good enough in 2026 to do what 2022-24 hardware couldn't. This is the single most impressive "unfinished business" in SIH history.

SIH 2023 · SIH1282 · Software⧗ Justice-tech RE-ASKED 2024 → 2026

Tech-Driven Solutions for Undertrial Prisoners

Ministry of Law & Justice

Why past attempts fell short

  • Solutions were informational portals/FAQ chatbots — nothing that moves a single prisoner closer to release
  • No linkage to the real bottleneck: identifying bail-eligible undertrials stuck for want of paperwork

What "complete" looks like

  • Eligibility screening engine over ePrisons-style records (offence, time served, Sec 436A BNSS) → auto-flag list for district legal services authority
  • Document checklist generator + status tracker per case; family/legal-aid matching workflow
  • Audit-grade privacy design (this is the part judges drilled teams on)
2026 echo: MHA is back with criminal-justice PS (SIH26189, SIH26190). A mature 2023-grade justice tool + 2026 polish = strong portfolio or finale entry.

SIH 2023 · SIH1284 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2024 (same PS)

Blockchain-Based eVault for Legal Records

Ministry of Law & Justice

Why past attempts fell short

  • "Blockchain for the sake of it" — token demos with no chain-of-custody logic courts actually need
  • No role model (judge/clerk/advocate/police), no sealing/unsealing workflow, no hash-verification UX a clerk can use

What "complete" looks like

  • Permissioned chain (Hyperledger Fabric) with evidence lifecycle: deposit→seal→transfer→view with immutable audit trail
  • Integration points with eCourts/DigiLocker; PDF hash-anchor and tamper-evidence viewer
  • Gas-free ops + recoverable keys for a government setting (why pure public-chain demos died)
2026 echo: SIH26190 — Secure digital document management for legal/investigation documents (MHA). Your completed eVault ≈ a head start on this year's finale.

SIH 2023 · SIH1357 · Software⧗ ASKED 2023 → 2024 → 2026 (3 editions)

Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Presence

Ministry of Commerce → NTRO

Why past attempts fell short

  • English-only VADER/TextBlob on tweets — useless on code-mixed Hinglish, the dominant register of Indian social media
  • Overall sentiment only; no aspect-level ("price good, delivery bad"), sarcasm, or coordinated-bot detection

What "complete" looks like

  • Multilingual code-mixed pipeline (IndicBERT/MuRIL) with aspect-based sentiment
  • Sarcasm + bot/coordination flags; trend narratives auto-summarized by an LLM with citations to posts
  • Live dashboard on a streaming source with evaluation numbers per language bucket
2026 echo: SIH26152 — Social Media Analytics (NTRO). Third time asked. A code-mixed, aspect-level engine is exactly what none of the 26052 submissions will have.

SIH 2023 · SIH1364 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2024 · feeds 2026 MHA PS

Fake Social Media Profile Detection & Reporting

Government of Gujarat

Why past attempts fell short

  • Random-forest on follower/following counts (Botometer-clone features) trained on synthetic data — collapses on real impersonation accounts
  • Zero treatment of impersonation rings and cloned-profile image matching
  • No actual "reporting" workflow — the second half of the PS

What "complete" looks like

  • Multimodal scoring: metadata + posted-image reuse (pHash/embeddings vs real user's photos) + posting-bursty graphs
  • Ring detection: cluster impersonators sharing infrastructure/templates
  • End-to-end: flag → evidence bundle → one-click reporting packet for platform/police formats
2026 echo: SIH26188 — Fake identity & document screening (MHA). Same DNA, bigger stage.

SIH 2024 · SIH1545 · Software⧗ NCB ask; theme returns 2026 (MHA cyber)

Identify Users Behind Drug Trafficking on Telegram / WhatsApp / Instagram

Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB)

Why past attempts fell short

  • Arguably the least-solved PS of 2024: encryption makes the naive ask impossible, so teams presented slideware
  • Nobody scoped the legally & technically possible subset: public channel/marketplace OSINT

What "complete" looks like

  • Realistic re-scope: monitor public Telegram channels/groups & hashtags; detect coded drug lexicon (emoji/slang NER) in Indic languages
  • Vendor-network graphs from public metadata; suspicious-account triage with evidence capture
  • Strict legal/privacy boundary documentation — judges killed teams precisely on this
2026 echo: MHA crypto-tracing trio (SIH26182–26184) is the same investigative-OSINT muscle. Complete this and you've built the hardest demo in that finale room.

SIH 2023 · SIH1453 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2024 → reborn ×3 in 2026 NTRO PS

Investigation of Vulnerabilities in Crypto Library Implementations

NTRO

Why past attempts fell short

  • Teams explained side-channel theory in slides; almost none shipped a tool that scans code/protocol configs and finds misuse
  • No coverage of the real-world failure mode: misconfiguration & deprecated primitives, not exotic math breaks

What "complete" looks like

  • Static analyzer (Semgrep rules + LLM triage) for crypto misuses in C/Java/Python: ECB mode, hard-coded keys, weak RNG, reused IVs
  • Protocol posture grader for TLS/IPsec configs with CVE cross-reference
  • Report with severity, exploit path and remediation patch-diff
2026 echo: SIH26159 (email crypto posture), SIH26160 (IPsec VPN analyzer), SIH26164 (Enterprise Crypto Discovery). NTRO asked this three times — they've never gotten the tool.

SIH 2023 · SIH1287 / SIH1288 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2024 (same PS)

Non-Revenue Water & Water-Footprint Assessment for Utilities

Ministry of Jal Shakti

Why past attempts fell short

  • Built water-footprint calculators (definitional web forms) — not an NRW loss-detection system
  • No DMA (district metered area) zone math, no night-line minimum-flow analysis, no leak localization from pressure/flow anomalies

What "complete" looks like

  • DMA-balance engine: inflow vs billed consumption → NRW% per zone, leakage hotspot ranking
  • Anomaly detection on flow/pressure series (synthetic series is fine — model a city rigorously)
  • Utility dashboard + prioritized repair worklist with ₹ recovered/year estimate
Context: Jal Shakti keeps returning to SIH (2025 INGRES chatbot etc.) — water-data tooling remains an open ministry need.

SIH 2023 · SIH1312 · Software⧗ Regulation-chatbot asked 2023 → 2026 (BIS ×2)

Hallucination-Proof Chatbot on Acts, Rules & Government Regulations

Ministry of Coal → (2026) BIS / Ministry of Consumer Affairs

Why past attempts fell short

  • Wrapped GPT around scraped PDFs; answers confidently wrong on section numbers — fatal for legal/regulatory use
  • No amendment-awareness: laws change; demos mixed repealed and current text
  • No refusal behavior on out-of-scope questions

What "complete" looks like

  • RAG with strict citation-anchoring: every sentence linked to act/section/clause; answers suppressed without retrieval support
  • Versioned corpus with amendment lineage + "as-on-date" filter
  • Bake-off eval set (100 officer-written Q&As) with citation-accuracy score published
2026 echo: SIH26107 & SIH26108 (BIS standards assistant). The ministry has asked for this 3 editions straight — still waiting for a non-hallucinating answer machine.

SIH 2023 · SIH1301 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2024 (same PS)

Official-Language English→Hindi Translation Tool (Gazette/Ministry Register)

Ministry of Power

Why past attempts fell short

  • Called Google/Bhashini APIs in a wrapper — failed on administrative terminology (संकल्पना-सpecific officialese), translating "concurrence", "despatch", "acquittance" absurdly
  • Destroyed document formatting (tables, headers, stamps) in PDF→PDF translation

What "complete" looks like

  • Terminology memory built from real bilingual govt documents (parallel corpus of official orders)
  • Layout-preserving pipeline: PDF in → PDF out with tables intact
  • Human-in-the-loop review UI with post-editing tracking (how ministries actually work)
Why it matters: Every Hindi-officer workflow in the country needs this; a layout-safe, terminology-true translator is a real product, not a demo.

SIH 2024 · SIH1647 · Software⧗ Agri-price asks return 2025 → 2026

AI/ML Price-Forecasting for Pulses & Vegetables (Onion, Potato)

Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

Why past attempts fell short

  • Trained an ARIMA/LSTM on an old Agmarknet CSV — demo worked, system died: no live data pipeline
  • Ignored what actually moves onion/tomato prices: arrivals, rain events, export bans, mandi strikes
  • Point forecasts with no confidence band = useless for a buffer-stock officer

What "complete" looks like

  • Live ingestion (Agmarknet/eNAM daily) with auto-retraining + drift alarm
  • Exogenous features: arrivals, weather, policy events; probabilistic forecast with P10/P90 bands
  • Officer view: "probability of breaching threshold in 30 days" trigger for intervention
2026 echo: SIH26132 (market linkage & price discovery, Maharashtra) — the price-intelligence core you build transfers directly.

SIH 2024 · SIH1621 · Software⧗ Crop-disease ask 2023 Kerala → 2024 → 2026 Maharashtra

AI-Driven Crop Disease Prediction & Management System

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Why past attempts fell short

  • PlantVillage-clone: 95% lab accuracy, collapses on farm photos (mud background, blur, mixed symptoms) — judges have seen this exact demo 500 times
  • Stopped at "this is leaf blight" with no treatment + dosage + locally-available product advisory

What "complete" looks like

  • Field-robust training: real farm-photo augmentation, out-of-distribution rejection ("not a disease / retake photo")
  • Advisory layer: cure + dose + nearest-input-dealer + cost, in the farmer's language
  • Outbreak early-warning: aggregate anonymized detections on a district map for the agri dept
2026 echo: SIH26131 (early detection of crop disease/pest, Maharashtra). Fourth edition asking. They want the field-grade one.

SIH 2023 · SIH1349 · Software⧗ RE-ASKED 2024 · MHA CCTV again 2026

Crowd & Crime Analytics on Existing CCTV Networks

Ministry of Railways → MHA (2026)

Why past attempts fell short

  • Demos ran on curated YouTube clips at 720p; real constraint was existing grainy, odd-angle station cameras and running on cheap edge boxes
  • Counted people; didn't flag precursors (density surge → stampede risk, loitering, wrong-direction flow, unattended baggage)

What "complete" looks like

  • Edge-inference design (INT8, Jetson-class budget) with multi-camera person re-ID across views
  • Event engine: crowd-density heat + surge-rate alerts, unattended-object, perimeter breach
  • Alert console with playback-snippet evidence + false-alarm feedback loop
2026 echo: SIH26187 — AI video analytics on existing CCTV for border surveillance (MHA). Third appearance of this ask.

SIH 2025 · SIH25070 · Software⧗ NTRO secure-erasure again 2026

Secure Data Wiping for Trustworthy IT-Asset Recycling

Ministry of Mines

Why past attempts fell short

  • Teams shipped a file-shredder script; the PS needs NIST 800-88 device-level sanitization with proof
  • No verification certificate, no handling of SSDs (wear-leveling defeats overwriting), no chain-of-custody for e-waste handlers

What "complete" looks like

  • Bootable wiper with per-drive profiles (ATA Secure Erase/crypto-erase for SSD, multi-pass for HDD), SMART verification
  • Signed tamper-evident sanitization certificate (QR-verifiable) + asset registry
  • Recovery-resistance test report (try carving your own wiped disk — show it fails)
2026 echo: SIH26149 — Secure data erasure + forensic file recovery tool (NTRO). Two years, same unmet need.

SIH 2025 · SIH25108 · Software⧗ Land-governance cluster exploded in 2026 (×8 PS)

FRA Atlas — Forest Rights Act WebGIS Decision-Support System

Ministry of Tribal Affairs

Why past attempts fell short

  • Built a pretty map viewer of public shapefiles; FRA is a legal workflow (claim→Gram Sabha→SDLC→DLC→title) — nobody mapped the claim lifecycle
  • No offline capability though target users are in zero-connectivity forest villages

What "complete" looks like

  • Claim-track system: GPS-tagged evidence collection offline → sync; status per claim at each committee level
  • GIS layers (forest cover, village boundaries, CFR potential area) with conflict-overlay analytics
  • Public dashboard of titles granted vs potential — the transparency outcome MoTA actually wants
2026 echo: Ministry of Rural Development's 8-PS land cluster (SIH26011–26019). Land records is this year's biggest ministry theme — and still unfinished.

How to use this list

  • For SIH 2026: Cards with a green "2026 echo" tell you which old problem has re-incarnated this year — studying the 2023–25 failure modes of that exact PS is free competitive intelligence nobody else in your finale room has.
  • For independent projects/startups: A ministry that asked 3 times is a validation of demand. Completing ISL translation, official-language translation, NRW analytics, or TB-grade crypto tooling is portfolio-and-startup grade work.
  • Execution rule: "Complete" = the gap column, not the original demo column. Ship the embarrassing edge cases (offline, Hindi, re-take-photo, verification certificates) — that's the difference between another SIH demo and the solution the ministry finally adopts.
